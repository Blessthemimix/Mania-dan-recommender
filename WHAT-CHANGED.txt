Dan recommender estimate update
===============================

Top-play chart estimates now account for the score's speed mods before
estimating chart difficulty. Previously, every score was analyzed at normal
speed, so a Half Time play could be shown as if it were played at 1.0x.

The osu! Legacy score importer now keeps the score's mod bit field. The
estimator uses 0.75x for Half Time, 1.5x for Double Time or Nightcore, and
1.0x when neither speed mod is present. The existing map estimator then
recalculates the chart at that rate. The profile calls these "chart estimates"
and still distinguishes them from verified Dan course clears and from
Mania Tracker's player-level skill estimate.

Existing saved estimates are refreshed when their score's rate was never
stored or has changed. Unchanged scores are not downloaded and recalculated
on every sync. New estimates save the applied rate with the score.

The osu! Legacy score feed exposes fixed HT/DT/Nightcore mods; arbitrary
custom rate adjustments are not available through that feed and are not
inferred here. Mania Tracker's own 4K RC/LN skill estimate remains displayed
separately as its independent, skill-based player estimate.

Course-clear status no longer treats a pass on one chart as a cleared Dan.
The score sync requires an explicit full-course marker, while the profile
shows Mania Tracker's course-clear evidence and separately recorded manual
proofs. Previously saved score-derived clears are retained as legacy,
unverified data and no longer drive the verified-clear/recommendation level.
Full-course clears use accuracy, not a miss cap: RC needs at least 96% stable
accuracy, and LN needs at least 97%, with any number of misses allowed if the
score is still a pass. The local osu! score feed provides stable judgment
counts. For Mania Tracker evidence, lazer judgment counts are converted to
stable accuracy using (300-point Perfect/Great, 200-point Good, 100-point Ok,
50-point Meh, 0-point Miss) divided by 300 times the total judgments. The
Tracker's stable accuracy is used only when its response identifies the score
as stable and omits judgment counts. Scores without a link or usable stable
accuracy, or below the track's threshold, are not verified clears. Rejected
tracker evidence also demotes a stale saved clear for that course, while
manual score proofs remain preserved. The high-tier Reform Zeta-through-Kappa
map entries are explicitly marked as full courses so their scores can be
checked by the automatic sync.

Tracker-imported clear records are now tracked separately from osu!-synced and
manual clears. When Mania Tracker successfully reports no qualifying course
score, an old Tracker-only clear is downgraded instead of lingering as a
verified clear; osu!-synced and manual clears are retained. The profile then
shows a personalized estimated Dan rank when available, rather than only
"none verified." The estimate uses Mania Tracker's per-user raw Dan value and
label band (for example, raw 9.72 / label 10- becomes 10th Dan Low), and keeps
each skillset's own estimate and clear evidence separate. These labels are
estimates, not claims of a course clear. Per-skillset Tracker evidence can also
show a Tracker clear or near clear independently of the overall Dan estimate.
LN estimates use numbered Dan names through 17th Dan (rather than RC Greek
tiers such as Alpha/Gamma); RC keeps its existing numbered-then-Greek naming.

The profile response is now a Discord embed with the osu! avatar as its
thumbnail. Its accent color is sampled from the avatar image; a logged
fallback color is used if the image cannot be downloaded or decoded.

The profile no longer reports the highest local per-map estimate as the
player's RC Dan. That max-only estimate produced outliers such as ETA High
for players whose tracker skill estimate is around 10-. The profile now uses
Mania Tracker's player-level RC estimate and reports its per-skillset levels
(Jack, Tech, Speed, and Stamina) as supporting context. For example, the
linked account mitix has a Jack estimate of 9 while its overall estimate is
10-, so one high chart estimate no longer overrides that skill breakdown.

Validation:
- Focused sync and profile tests cover the RC 96% and LN 97% accuracy bars,
  scores with misses, stale-clear demotion, and lazer-to-stable conversion.
- Profile tests cover personalized low/mid estimates from different tracker
  values and fallback display when no verified course score is available.
- Course tests verify that single-map scores do not count as Dan course clears.
- Profile tests cover the embed, course evidence, and avatar-color sampling.
- One existing numericToDanLevel test still expects eta-high for 25 while the
  current mapping returns kappa-high.
- Restart the bot to load the updated source.
